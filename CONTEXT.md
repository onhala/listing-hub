# Listing Hub

Listing Hub is a domain context for managing the complete lifecycle of marketplace listings across regional classifieds portals through multimodal AI analysis, competitive market radar, and supervised browser automation. It enables friction-free private selling with strict human-in-the-loop validation, automated inventory hygiene, and market-driven pricing.

## Language

### Entities & Lifecycle

**Listing**:
An aggregate root representing a single commercial offering across one or more marketplace portals, holding descriptive copy, pricing, categorized media, and tracking metadata.
_Avoid_: Ad, advert, product, item, record

**ListingStatus**:
The lifecycle phase of a listing representing its operational availability, publication state, or archival condition.
_Avoid_: State, stage, condition

**Draft**:
A listing that is assembled locally with text copy, photo assets, and pricing recommendations, but has not yet been submitted to any marketplace portal.
_Avoid_: Concept, staging, unpublished item

**Active**:
A listing that is verified, live, and publicly discoverable on one or more external marketplace portals.
_Avoid_: Published, live, online

**NeedsReview**:
A listing that has completed form prefilling or encountered an authentication barrier (such as SMS verification) and is paused awaiting human visual sign-off or intervention.
_Avoid_: Pending, suspended, blocked, ready_for_review

**Sold**:
A concluded listing where the item was successfully handed over to a buyer, retained for historical accounting and profit reporting.
_Avoid_: Closed, archived, completed

**Expired**:
A listing whose standard publication duration on a marketplace portal has lapsed without renewal, rendering it invisible to buyers.
_Avoid_: Stale, dead, out-of-date

**MarketRadarAnalysis**:
A structured market valuation report generated by scanning competitive listings across classified portals and synthesizing pricing distributions with AI reasoning.
_Avoid_: Price check, scraping report, competitor scan

**SubdomainCategory**:
The portal-specific thematic category and network routing domain required to preserve form state and direct a listing to its correct portal partition.
_Avoid_: Rubrika, section, portal topic

**TwoPhaseSession**:
A supervised browser execution lifecycle that separates automated form population from explicit human review and final submission confirmation.
_Avoid_: Automation job, robot task, browser run

**PhotoAsset**:
A photographic digital asset associated with a listing, evaluated for visual clarity, framing orientation, and sequence priority.
_Avoid_: Image, picture, snapshot, thumbnail

### Value Objects

**PricePoint**:
An immutable monetary figure and currency designation representing an asking price, realized sale price, or baseline boundary.
_Avoid_: Cost, amount, fee

**PriceRecommendation**:
A tri-level pricing structure derived from competitive distribution, offering Quick Sale, Fair Market, and Premium valuation targets.
_Avoid_: Price tiers, pricing suggestions, valuation quote

**ContactProfile**:
An immutable specification of seller identity coordinates, postal routing parameters, and standard delivery terms.
_Avoid_: User profile, account info, seller settings

### Operations

**DraftAd**:
The creation of a new uncommitted listing aggregate initialized with synthesized copy, categorized media, and preliminary metadata.
_Avoid_: Create ad, make listing, post draft

**AnalyzePhotos**:
The multimodal visual inspection of photo assets to extract physical specifications, detect defects, evaluate completeness, and suggest catalog titles.
_Avoid_: Scan photos, inspect images, run vision

**ProbeMarketRadar**:
The sampling of competitive listings across active marketplace portals to calculate median valuations and generate pricing recommendations.
_Avoid_: Scrape prices, spy competitors, market check

**LaunchTwoPhasePosting**:
The initiation of a supervised automation workflow that navigates to the target portal domain and prefills listing details up to the confirmation gate.
_Avoid_: Publish ad, run robot, push listing

**ConfirmSubmission**:
The explicit human verification step acknowledging that an ad has been submitted on the portal, capturing the live external identifier and promoting the listing to Active.
_Avoid_: Finalize, accept ad, approve

**CancelAction**:
The immediate termination of an ongoing automation session, releasing remote resources while leaving the local listing state intact.
_Avoid_: Abort, kill, stop

**RepostWithNewPrice**:
The reactivation and price adjustment of an existing or expired listing on a marketplace portal to renew visibility at an updated price point.
_Avoid_: Re-list, bump, top-up

**PurgeListing**:
The permanent removal of a listing record from local persistence, accompanied by optional de-listing from external portals and localized photo asset cleanup.
_Avoid_: Delete ad, remove item, wipe listing
